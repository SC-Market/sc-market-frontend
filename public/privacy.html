5. DO WE USE COOKIES AND OTHER TRACKING TECHNOLOGIES?

In Short: We use cookies to keep you signed in and to remember your choices. Analytics only run if you click "Accept".

We use cookies and similar technologies (like your browser's local storage) to access or store information:

  • Sign-in cookies (essential): a session cookie and a refresh token cookie that last 60 days and an access token cookie that lasts 15 minutes keep you signed in, and short-lived cookies (10 minutes) protect the sign-in process with Discord and Citizen iD.
  • Preferences (essential): cookies and local storage remember your cookie choice (for 1 year), theme, language, cart, selected organization, pinned navigation items, and similar settings.
  • Analytics (optional): only if you click "Accept", Google Analytics sets cookies to measure how the website is used, and BugSnag Performance measures how fast pages load (without storing an identifier for you or sending page addresses). If you click "Necessary only", or make no choice, neither runs.
  • Error reporting (essential, anonymous): when something breaks, our website, servers, and Discord bot send an error report to BugSnag. Error reports contain the error, the page or API route where it happened (without query strings), the recent pages visited in that tab, and basic browser and device information. They do not contain your IP address, your account, or any identifier for you or your browser, and BugSnag keeps them for 7 days.

When you first visit, our cookie banner asks for your choice. To change it later, clear the "cookie_consent" cookie for sc-market.space in your browser, and the banner will ask again.

CCPA Privacy Notice

The California Code of Regulations defines a "resident" as:

  • (1) every individual who is in the State of California for other than a temporary or transitory purpose and
  • (2) every individual who is domiciled in the State of California who is outside the State of California for a temporary or transitory purpose

All other individuals are defined as "non-residents."

If this definition of "resident" applies to you, we must adhere to certain rights and obligations regarding your personal information.
