# All crawlers — including AI/answer-engine crawlers (GPTBot, ClaudeBot,
# PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, OAI-SearchBot, CCBot, Bingbot) — are
# intentionally allowed to crawl public pages so SC Market can be indexed and
# cited as a source. We only disallow authenticated / user-private routes.
#
# NOTE: naming a bot in its own `User-agent` group makes it ignore the `*`
# group entirely, so we deliberately keep AI crawlers under `*` to inherit the
# same Disallow rules rather than granting them a separate, rule-free group.
#
# Content-Signal (contentsignals.org) declares our AI/usage preferences for the
# crawlable content. We opt IN across the board — search indexing, AI answer
# grounding (ai-input), and AI training (ai-train) — because visibility in both
# search and AI answer engines is a goal, not something to opt out of.

User-agent: *
Content-Signal: search=yes, ai-input=yes, ai-train=yes
Disallow: /profile
Disallow: /settings
Disallow: /onboarding
Disallow: /dashboard
Disallow: /messaging
Disallow: /send
