SC Market is the Star Citizen marketplace where players buy, sell, and trade in-game items and hire orgs for services like hauling, escort, medical, and salvage, all in aUEC.

## How it works

Sellers open a shop and list what they have. Buyers send offers from their cart, and once an offer is accepted, both players meet in game to hand over the item and the aUEC. Buyers and sellers review each other after their orders, so reputation builds up over time. The [Help & Guides](/guides) walk through each step.

## Fees

SC Market is free to use, and no aUEC passes through us. Buyers pay sellers directly in game.

Every trade on SC Market is in aUEC. Real-money trading isn't allowed, in line with CIG's Terms of Service.

## Is it safe?

Trades happen between players, so we can't guarantee them, but buyers and sellers review each other after every order. Check a seller's ratings and order history before you trade.

## For organizations

Orgs can run their own shop on SC Market and sell as a team. For recruiting and member management, use our sister site [SC Orgs](https://sc-orgs.space). Org storefronts can also get a custom domain on request: [contact us on Discord](https://discord.com/invite/N4Gy8py8J4).
