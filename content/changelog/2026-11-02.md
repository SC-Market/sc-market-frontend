## A new homepage

The homepage is built around the market: a live feed of new listings, what players want right now, top rated shops and this week's trading stats, with search front and centre.

## Notifications

- All your notification settings are on one **Notifications** page in Settings, with tabs and a switch per channel (site, push, email, Discord DM).
- Mute an org's notifications without leaving it.
- Get notified when someone joins your org and when a new public contract is posted. New public contracts can also go to your webhooks.
- Push notifications and emails open the page they're about.

## Price watchlist

Manage your price watchlist on the site, and use the new **Watch price** buttons to add an item to it.

## Market, orders and shops

- When creating or renaming a shop, you see right away whether the name is free, with suggestions if it isn't.
- Auctions highlight your own bid, and the highest bid is marked correctly.
- Sellers in personal shops get the Stock Allocation tab on their orders, and stock is reserved lot by lot.
- Buy order cards link to the buy order, and orders without a price say so.
- Pending org invites have a Decline button next to Join.
- In the game database, item pages list the in-game shops that sell the item, and the blueprint filters (Has Mission Source, Hide Unsourceable) cover every blueprint, not just the loaded page.

## API tokens

You can create API tokens for the public API from Settings. The [API tokens guide](/guides/discord/api-tokens) explains how.

## Faster pages

The homepage, game database, blueprints, missions, guides and About now arrive ready to read, so they show up much sooner, especially on slow connections. Fonts load without a flash, the light theme no longer flashes dark, and a first visit in another language renders in that language straight away.

## Translations

German, Spanish, French, Japanese, Ukrainian and Chinese (Simplified and Traditional) now cover the whole site.

## Also

- Cookie preferences are in Settings and the footer, and the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy have been rewritten in plain language.
- Many buttons, switches and menus have clearer labels for screen readers.
- The contractors page and org pages work better on phones.
