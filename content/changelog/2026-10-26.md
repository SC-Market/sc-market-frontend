## Help & Guides

SC Market now has its own [Help & Guides](/guides): step-by-step guides on creating an account, buying, selling, following an order, running an org storefront and connecting the Discord bot. A directory on every guide page lets you jump between them, and the new [About](/about) page explains what SC Market is.

## Price alerts

Settings has a new **Price Alerts** tab. Choose how you hear about a watched item dropping in price: on the site, by push notification, by email or by Discord DM.

## Market

- Sidebar filters apply as soon as you pick them, and Item Type accepts any text you type.
- The Items / Services / Open Contracts tab strip is gone; use the sidebar to switch.
- Sort By on buy orders now changes the order of the results.
- Fulfilling a buy order asks you to pick one of your listings.

## Orgs and shops

- Filter your org's audit log by member username.
- Org management tabs and buttons only show when your role can use them, and the unused Market, Blocked Users and Customers tabs are gone.
- White-label sites follow the org's Sidebar Tabs settings and custom tabs.
- Creating a shop now saves its tags, languages and custom order settings.
- New recruiting posts are created for the org selected in the sidebar.
- Members with Claim Orders can claim offers on the site.

## Discord bot

The site's Discord pages, guides and settings now describe the bot's commands and the permissions it needs.

## Smoother loading

Pages show a placeholder in the shape of the real content while they load, instead of a spinner followed by a blank page. Background refreshes keep what you're looking at on screen, and dashboard widgets fit their space on narrow screens.

## Also

- The homepage has fresh copy, new feature sections and more FAQs.
- Mining ore prices ignore placeholder listings, so they are closer to what ore really sells for.
