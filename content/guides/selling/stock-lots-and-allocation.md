Stock on SC Market is kept in lots: a quantity of one item variant, with an optional location and quality. A listing sells from its lots, so buyers can only order what the lots hold. You manage lots on your shop's **Stock** page.

## Before you start

- A shop with at least one listing ([sell items](/guides/selling/sell-items)).
- In an org shop: the org's **Manage Market** permission to change lots.

## Steps

1. Switch the sidebar to your shop and open **Stock**. The page opens on **Manage Inventory**.
2. Find the lot in **All Stock Lots**. Use **Search lots...** or the **Listing** filter to narrow the list.
3. Edit the lot's **Quantity** or **Location (optional)** in the grid.
4. To add stock, click **Add Lot**, pick the **Listing**, enter the **Quantity** and the lot's quality, and click **Create Lot**.
5. To remove a lot you no longer have, click its delete icon.

> [!TIP]
> To give a listing several lots, for example the same item at two locations or in two qualities, add them under **More options** › **Stock Lots** › **Add Stock Lot** when you create the listing.

## Listed and Personal lots

Each lot's **Status** is **Listed** or **Personal**. Listed stock counts toward what buyers can order. Personal stock is yours but isn't for sale.

## What happens when you sell

When you accept an offer, the ordered quantity comes out of a listed lot, so buyers can't order stock you've already sold. If the order is cancelled, the quantity goes back to the lot. That's the default **Automatic (FIFO)** allocation mode; to pick lots yourself, choose **Manual selection** under **Allocation mode** in your shop's **Settings**. **Allocated Stock** on the Stock page lists lots that have stock reserved for open orders.

## Your personal inventory

[My Inventory](/inventory) lists every lot you own, across your shops. Use **Add Lot** there to track items you aren't selling yet: new lots start unlisted. **Unlink from listing** takes a lot off its listing without deleting it.
