A buy order is a buyer's request for an item: the quantity they want and the price range they'll pay. If you list that item in your shop, you can fill the request, fully or in part. Filling it creates the order right away, without an offer step.

## Before you start

- A [verified account](/guides/getting-started/create-an-account).
- An active listing of the same item, with enough listed stock, in a shop you own personally.

## Steps

1. Open [Buy Orders](/buyorders) and find a request for an item you sell. Your dashboard also lists **Buy Orders You Can Fulfill**.
2. Open the buy order. Check what's **Offering**, the **Quantity** remaining and any **Quality Requirements**.
3. Under **Can you supply this?**, click **Fulfill Order**.
4. On the item page, click **Fulfill**. If the buyer accepts partial fills, enter the **Quantity to fulfill**.
5. Confirm. SC Market creates the order and opens it.

## What happens next

- The order uses your listing's price, and its quantity comes out of your listed stock.
- When the full quantity is filled, the buy order closes.
- Continue as with any order: see [handle orders as a seller](/guides/orders/handle-orders-as-a-seller).
