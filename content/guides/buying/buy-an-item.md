To buy an item, add it to your cart and submit an offer to the seller. When the seller accepts, the offer becomes an order and you meet in game to pay in aUEC and collect the item. No aUEC passes through SC Market.

## Before you start

- A [verified account](/guides/getting-started/create-an-account). You can browse without one, but the cart needs it.

## Steps

1. Search the [market](/market). Narrow the results with **Item Type**, the price range and **Pickup Method**, and use **Sort By** to order them.
2. Open a listing and click **Add** next to the variant you want (**Add to Cart** on mobile).
3. Choose the **Quantity** and click **Add to Cart**. The quantity is limited by the seller's stock and order limits.
4. Open your [cart](/market/cart). Items are grouped by seller.
5. Optionally write a **Note** to the seller, then click **Submit Offer** for that seller and **Confirm Order**.
6. Your offer opens. Wait for the seller to accept it, or to send a counter offer.

> [!NOTE]
> You submit one offer per seller. If your cart has items from three sellers, you check out three times.

## Bid on an auction

Auction listings have a bid panel showing the **Current Bid** and when it **Ends In**. Enter **Your Bid (aUEC)**, at least the **Min Bid** shown, and click **Place Bid**. When the auction ends, the highest bid wins and SC Market creates an offer for the winner at that price. If nobody bids, or the seller's reserve price isn't met, the listing expires.

## Post a buy order

Can't find what you need? [Create a buy order](/buyorder/create) with the item, quantity and price range you'll pay. Sellers can fill it from their listings, which creates an order with you directly. Browse open requests on [Buy Orders](/buyorders).

## Wait for a better price

Too expensive, or out of stock? Click **Watch price** on the item or listing and set your target price. You get an alert when someone lists it at or below that price: see [get price alerts with the watchlist](/guides/buying/price-alerts-watchlist).

## What happens next

Your offers and orders are in [My Orders](/orders). Some sellers only take offers from buyers who have set their [availability](/availability), so set it if checkout asks you to.

> [!WARNING]
> Trades happen between players, so SC Market can't guarantee them. Check the seller's ratings and reviews before you trade.
