Every org on SC Market has a storefront: its page at `sc-market.space/contractor/<your Spectrum ID>`. It opens on your shop, featured listings, services, trust stats (completed orders, rating, members), reviews and, if your org is on SC Orgs, a recruiting card. It's free, and it stays public.

White-label puts that storefront on your org's own address, `yourorg.sc-market.space`, styled with your org's colors and logo. Visitors browse, buy and talk to your team there with their normal SC Market account.

## Set up your storefront

Open your org's manage area and the **Storefront** tab. The **Set up your storefront** checklist has six steps, each with a button that takes you there:

1. **Verify your org through its RSI page**.
2. **Open the org's shop**.
3. **Add your first listing**.
4. **Connect Discord, so orders land in your server**.
5. **Bring in 3 or more members**.
6. **Unlock branding**: done once your org meets the criteria below. **See what's missing** shows what's left.

## What branding unlocks

Branding unlocks by itself once your org meets three criteria, and stays unlocked while it does:

- **RSI-verified org**: registered through its RSI org page.
- At least 3 members on SC Market.
- A listing or an order in the last 30 days.

The **Storefront** tab shows each one and what's still missing. SC Market staff can also unlock branding for an org. It adds:

- **Your theme**: colors for dark and light mode, card and nav styles, corner rounding and a favicon. See [customize your white-label theme](/guides/org-storefront/white-label-theme).
- **On your subdomain**: your logo and name in the sidebar, page titles with your org's name, and a market that only shows your org's listings.
- **Site settings**: make the subdomain public or members-only, and pick the page visitors land on.
- **Sidebar tabs**: hide the market sections you don't use, and add your own links to the sidebar.
- **The Manage Theme permission**: it appears in your role editor, so you can choose who edits the look.

Everything else, such as listings, orders and stock, works the same as on sc-market.space.

## Get your subdomain

Subdomains are `yourorg.sc-market.space`. Staff set them up:

1. Fill in **Enquire about a branded storefront** in your org's **Storefront** tab (members with **Manage Org Details**), or in your shop's settings for a shop.
2. Give the subdomain you'd like (usually your Spectrum ID), what you have in mind, and how we should reach you: the Discord account linked to your profile, or a message on SC Market.
3. The form then shows your enquiry's status while we set up the address with you. One enquiry per org or shop is open at a time.
4. When staff accept or decline it, you get a notification, and an email unless you turned **Enquiry accepted or declined** off in your [notification settings](/guides/getting-started/notification-settings).

Only `sc-market.space` subdomains are available for now, not domains of your own.

## What visitors see

- Your theme and favicon. On sc-market.space your org's pages keep the standard SC Market look.
- When they open the home page, your chosen landing page, otherwise your org's page. In members-only mode they go to their **Assigned to Me** page instead.
- Search engines are pointed at the matching sc-market.space page, so your org's page there keeps the ranking. Members-only mode applies only on your subdomain: your storefront on sc-market.space stays public.
- In members-only mode, a sign-in screen with your org's name. They sign in with Discord or Citizen iD, just like on sc-market.space.
- **Help & Guides** and **About** in the sidebar and footer open on sc-market.space in a new tab. The guides always live on the official site.

## What only works on sc-market.space

For account safety, some actions are blocked on white-label addresses. Do them on [sc-market.space](https://sc-market.space) instead:

- API tokens, sign-in sessions and two-factor settings.
- Email, linked accounts, data export and push notification setup.
- Org roles, org ownership transfer, shop transfer and webhooks.

## Who can do what

- Ask for a subdomain and change the directory setting: members with **Manage Org Details** (for a shop, its managers). SC Market staff set the subdomain up.
- Edit the theme: members with **Manage Theme**. The Owner and Admin roles have it.
- Change the site settings and sidebar tabs: members with **Manage Org Details**.

Each section of the tab only shows to members with the permission it needs, so a member with just one of the two sees only their part.

## Advanced

### The storefront directory

The [Storefronts](/storefronts) page and the home page list RSI-verified orgs with at least one public listing or an active service. Orgs that only offer services get a **Services** tag. Orgs whose listings, services and managers have all gone quiet are listed last, not hidden.

**Show in the public storefront directory** in the **Storefront** tab takes your org out of it, and the same switch in an org shop's **Branded storefront** settings takes that shop out. The directory lists orgs, so personal shops aren't in it.

Members-only storefronts are never listed: if your subdomain is set to members-only, the setting stays off. Your branded subdomain still works for your members, and members-only listings don't count as public listings.

### Put your storefront on your own site

Show your storefront on your org's own website:

1. In the **Storefront** tab, open **Get embed code**.
2. Under **Widget**, pick **Storefront card** (your org's name, rating and stats) or **Listings** (your public listings). **Preview** shows how it looks.
3. Click **Copy code** and paste the code into your website.

The widget is read-only and always shows your current public listings and stats. Every link in it opens SC Market in a new tab.
