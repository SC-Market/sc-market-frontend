White-label runs SC Market on your org's own web address, such as `yourorg.sc-market.space` or a domain you own, styled with your org's colors. Visitors browse, buy and talk to your team there with their normal SC Market account. It isn't self-serve: we set it up for your org on request.

## What the white-label tier unlocks

White-label is SC Market's premium tier for orgs. It adds:

- **Your own address**: a subdomain of `sc-market.space`, or your own domain.
- **Your theme**: colors for dark and light mode, card and nav styles, corner rounding and a favicon. See [customize your white-label theme](/guides/org-storefront/white-label-theme).
- **Site settings**: make the site public or members-only, and pick the page visitors land on.
- **The Manage Theme permission**: it appears in your role editor, so you can choose who edits the look.

Everything else, such as listings, orders and stock, works the same as on sc-market.space.

## Subdomain or custom domain

- **Subdomain** (`yourorg.sc-market.space`): the quickest option. You don't need a domain of your own.
- **Custom domain** (for example `shop.yourorg.com`): you need to own the domain and be able to change its DNS settings.

## Request white-label

1. Open the **Support** link in the site footer. It takes you to the SC Market Discord.
2. Tell us your org's Spectrum ID and the subdomain or domain you want.
3. We turn on white-label for the org and set up the address with you. Only SC Market staff can do this.

Once it's on, members with **Manage Theme** see a **Theme & White Label** tab in the org's manage area.

## What visitors see

- Your theme and favicon. On sc-market.space your org's pages keep the standard SC Market look.
- When they open the home page, your chosen landing page, otherwise your org's page. In members-only mode they go to their **Assigned to Me** page instead.
- In members-only mode, a sign-in screen with your org's name. They sign in with Discord or Citizen iD, just like on sc-market.space.
- **Help & Guides** and **About** in the sidebar and footer open on sc-market.space in a new tab. The guides always live on the official site.

## What only works on sc-market.space

For account safety, some actions are blocked on white-label addresses. Do them on [sc-market.space](https://sc-market.space) instead:

- API tokens, sign-in sessions and two-factor settings.
- Email, linked accounts, data export and push notification setup.
- Org roles, org ownership transfer, shop transfer and webhooks.

## Who can do what

- Turn on white-label and set the domain: SC Market staff, on request.
- Edit the theme: members with **Manage Theme**. The Owner and Admin roles have it.
- Change the site settings and sidebar tabs: **Manage Theme** to open the tab, plus **Manage Org Details** to save.
