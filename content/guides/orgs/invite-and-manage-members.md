Invite a player by their SC Market username, or share an invite link that anyone can use to join. Both are on your org's **Invites** tab. Everyone who joins gets the org's **Member** role, and you can change their roles afterwards.

## Before you start

- The org's **Manage Invites** permission to invite.
- The player has an SC Market account.

## Steps

1. Open [My Organizations](/my-orgs), click **Manage** on your org and select the **Invites** tab.
2. Under **Invite Members**, pick the player's **Username**, add an optional **Note**, and click **Submit**.
3. The player gets a "Contractor invite from" notification on SC Market. They open it and click **Accept** (or **Decline**). A **Join Organization** button also shows on your org's page for them.

> [!NOTE]
> Invite one player per submission. If you pick several usernames at once, only the first one currently gets the notification.

## Share an invite link

1. On the **Invites** tab, under **Create New Invite Code**, set **Max Invite Uses**. 0 means unlimited.
2. Click **Submit**. The code appears in the **Invites** list with how often it was used.
3. Click the copy icon next to the code to copy the invite link, and share it on Discord or in game.
4. The player opens the link, signs in and clicks **Accept** under **Accept Contractor Invite**.

Invite codes don't expire. Click the delete icon next to a code to stop it working.

## Give members roles

New members only get **Member**. To give someone more permissions, open the **Roles and Members** tab and pick roles in their **Roles** selector. See [roles and permissions](/guides/orgs/roles-and-permissions).

## Remove a member

On the **Roles and Members** tab, click **Kick** on the member's row. You need the **Kick Members** permission, and you can't kick someone ranked above you. Kicking removes all their org roles.

## Leave an org

Open [Settings](/settings), select **Contractors** and click **Leave Organization** next to the org. The owner can't leave: use **Transfer Ownership** there first.

## Advanced

- **One link for a recruiting drive**: create a code with **Max Invite Uses** at 0, post it with your [recruiting post](/guides/orgs/recruiting), and delete it when the drive ends.
- **Find members fast**: the **Members** list on **Roles and Members** has **Search** and a **Role** filter.
- **Export the roster**: click **Export** above the **Members** list to download the members' usernames as a CSV file.
- **Track who joined**: every invite, join, kick and role change is in the org's [audit log](/guides/orgs/audit-logs).

## What happens next

New members see your org in their sidebar's context switcher and in [My Organizations](/my-orgs). With the right roles they can work on the org's orders and shop.
