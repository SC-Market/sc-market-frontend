Open [Register an Org](/org/register), enter your RSI org's Spectrum ID and prove you run it by adding a code to the org's RSI page. You become the org's owner on SC Market, and its name, logo, banner and introduction are copied from RSI.

## Before you start

- An SC Market account ([create one](/guides/getting-started/create-an-account)).
- Edit rights on your org's page on the RSI website.
- The org isn't registered on SC Market yet. Each Spectrum ID can be registered once.

## Steps

1. Open [Register an Org](/org/register) and stay on the **Existing RSI Org** tab.
2. Enter the org's **Org Spectrum ID**. You can also paste the org's RSI page URL: the ID is taken from it.
3. Click **Copy Code and Open Org Page**. This copies your personal code and opens the org's admin page on the RSI website.
4. On RSI, paste the code into the org's introduction, history, manifesto or charter, and save.
5. Back on SC Market, click **Submit** once the code shows on the public org page.

> [!TIP]
> You can remove the code from your RSI page once the org is registered.

## Set up the org profile

Your org's profile is the page players see at **Org Page**. To edit it, open [My Organizations](/my-orgs), click **Manage** on the org, and use the **About** tab (it needs the **Manage Org Details** permission):

- **General**: the **Organization Name** and a Markdown **Description**.
- **Categories & Tags**: **Org Tags** such as mining or transport. Players filter the org directory and recruiting posts by these.
- **Appearance**: **Upload Logo** (PNG, JPEG or WebP, under 1 MB) and **Set new banner** (under 2.5 MB).

Click **Save Changes** when you're done. Languages are on the **Settings** tab: pick every language your members speak under **Supported Languages** and click **Save Languages**. Players can filter orgs by language.

## Create an org without an RSI page

For a group that has no RSI org, use the **New Contractor** tab instead:

1. Enter a **Contractor Name** (up to 50 characters).
2. Enter a **Contractor Identifier**: letters and numbers only, up to 30. SC Market adds `~` in front, so `MYCREW` becomes `~MYCREW`. It can't be changed later.
3. Add a **Description**, a **Logo** and **Banner** image URL, and the **Supported Languages (Optional)**.
4. Click **Submit**. SC Market opens your new org's page.

## Connect Discord

To get a Discord thread for each order and alerts your team can claim, connect the SC Market bot to your org's server. See [connect the Discord bot](/guides/discord/connect-the-discord-bot).

## What happens next

You get the **Owner** role. The org also starts with an **Admin** and a **Member** role, and new members get **Member**. Invite your members next, then open a shop so the org can sell. To find new members, we recommend [recruiting on SC Orgs](/guides/org-storefront/recruiting).
